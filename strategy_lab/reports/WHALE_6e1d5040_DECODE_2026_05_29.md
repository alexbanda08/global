## 6. Strategy Verdict

### What IS the $935k strategy?

**A systematic BTC price-level probability market maker on Polymarket's NegRisk framework.**

Two complementary legs:

1. **High-probability outcome accumulation (buy & hold):** Buys large size of near-certain outcomes (e.g., "Bitcoin WON'T dip to $65k") at $0.90-0.99 per share, holds to expiry and redeems at $1.00. Low risk, low return per dollar (~1-10%) but the $300k+ position sizes generate significant absolute dollars.

2. **Low-probability outcome selling (market making):** Posts resting limit orders as maker to sell unlikely outcomes (e.g., "Bitcoin WILL reach $120k") at $0.07-0.10 per share. Collects premium from speculators willing to pay for extreme payoffs. These are the NegRisk composite fills at 7.5 cents median price. Generates ~$593k net in 27 days from 57k fills.

### Is it pair-arb / mint-merge?

The 166 MERGE events seen in the initial classification ARE present — the wallet does merge Yes+No pairs occasionally — but it is a **minor activity** (244 arb txs, $3,673 deployed). The core PnL is from the two legs above, not traditional mint→merge arbitrage.

### Risk profile

- **Not risk-free.** The wallet has directional exposure: "bitcoin_dip" category has -$59k realized PnL, "bitcoin_reach" has -$182k realized PnL. These are cases where the wallet bought the likely outcome but BTC moved against it.
- The winning categories (bitcoin_above: +$214k, bitcoin_range: +$71k, up-down: +$100k) suggest the overall book is net-positive with BTC roughly holding its level.
- **Main risk:** if BTC makes a large unexpected move (crashes to $65k or surges to $90k), the positions in "WON'T dip to $65k" or "WON'T reach $90k" would incur heavy losses.

## 7. Replicability Assessment

### Can we replicate this?

**Partially yes, with caveats:**

| Component | Replicable? | Notes |
|-----------|-------------|-------|
| High-prob outcome buying | Yes | Simply buy "No" to extreme BTC price levels at $0.90-0.99, hold to expiry. Low edge per trade but compoundable. |
| NegRisk low-prob market making | Yes | Post resting sell orders for long-shot outcomes at $0.07-0.10. Capital-light per position but needs constant monitoring. |
| Scale | No (yet) | Requires $500k–$1.5M working capital to match this wallet's daily output. |
| Information edge | Unknown | The wallet appears to calibrate probability accurately (net profitable) but it's unclear if it uses external models or just exploits retail sentiment extremes. |
| Pair-arb | Yes but tiny | The 244-tx arb was only $3k USDC — too small to be the main driver. |

### Key replication challenges:

1. **Capital:** Need $500k+ USDC locked on Polymarket simultaneously
2. **Market selection:** Must identify which BTC level markets are mispriced (e.g., "dip to $65k" at $0.90 is good if current BTC is $108k; at $0.95 if BTC is $85k it's different)
3. **Inventory management:** 200 concurrent positions need monitoring for resolution
4. **Position sizing:** The wallet invests $100k-$300k per single "high-probability" position — requires conviction and large float

### Bottom line:

This is a **low-risk, capital-intensive probability arbitrage strategy** on BTC price outcome markets. The edge is:
- Selling overpriced long-shots to retail speculators (the NegRisk market-making leg)
- Buying underpriced near-certain outcomes and collecting at expiry

It IS replicable conceptually, but requires $500k+ capital and a robust position tracking / probability calibration system. NOT deployable with our current <$10k test budget. The strategy scales with capital — the wallet's $935k lifetime profit used roughly $1-2M average float, implying ~50-100% annual return on capital.
