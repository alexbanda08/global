# F2 OOS + Cross-Exchange Basis Hypothesis — REJECTED

**Date:** 2026-06-04 · Re-test of `F2_FINAL_VERDICT_2026_05_18.md` §5 Phase-1 with new data.
**Scripts:** `strategy_lab/f2_replica/runner_v2.py` (trigger OOS) + `f2_basis_oos_2026_06_04.py` (basis join)
**Data:** fresh window **May 30 22:00 → Jun 4 21:00 UTC** — the only window where canonical has BOTH
Polymarket books/trades AND the new `cex_futures_ticker` (mark/index/funding/OI; bybit/okx/gate/bitget BTC perp).
**One line:** The F2 trigger has **no edge OOS** on the broad universe (−$0.18/tr, t=−6.19), and a
cross-exchange **basis-dislocation gate does NOT recover an edge — it is mildly *harmful*.** §5 Phase-1
hypothesis ("F2 fires when basis is dislocated") is **rejected**. F2's slug-selector is not basis.

## What was run
1. **Trigger OOS** — `runner_v2.py --asset BTC --tf 5m` over the new window, broad gate
   (n_trades_5s≥50, |flow|≥0.1, sum_asks≥1.005, offset≥60s). 1323 slugs → **4359 fires**.
   - Result: WR 28.0%, **$/tr −$0.177**, total **−$771** (@\$1 stake; −$19.3k @ \$25).
   - Config B (the "86% WR" config: n≥100, |flow|≥0.3, sum_asks≥1.01, offset≥120): n=73, WR 26%, −$0.18/tr.
   - → The 85.75% WR was **in-sample slug-selection survivorship**; the trigger formula carries no edge
     OOS, exactly as the broad-universe section of the original verdict predicted.
2. **Basis join** — merge_asof (≤10s) each fire to all 4 exchanges' ticker; features:
   `xbasis_bp` (cross-exchange mark dispersion), `markidx_bp` (perp premium), `fund_abs` (|funding|).
   99.9% fire coverage.

## Basis-dislocation gate (on Config-B fires) — the hypothesis test
| gate | n | WR | $/tr | t |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| xbasis ≥ p70 (dislocated) | 22 | 0.136 | **−0.737** | −4.51 |
| xbasis ≥ p80 (dislocated) | 15 | 0.067 | **−0.787** | −3.81 |
| xbasis < p50 (calm) | 36 | 0.389 | +0.177 | 0.59 |
| markidx ≥ p80 (premium) | 15 | 0.200 | −0.390 | −1.07 |
| fund_abs ≥ p80 | 15 | 0.267 | −0.412 | −1.44 |

- **Wider cross-exchange basis → strictly worse** trades (WR collapses to 6.7–13.6%). Dislocation is an
  *avoid* signal, not the edge — intuitive: wide basis = underlying in motion = adverse lag-taker pricing.
- "Calm basis" is mildly positive (+$0.18/tr) but tiny n and **not significant** (t=0.59).
- Best of 9 basis gates: per-fire Sharpe negative; **DSR prob 0.000, not significant**.

## Verdict
- **Basis hypothesis rejected.** The cross-exchange futures data — the §5 Phase-1 "priority" missing piece —
  does not reproduce or explain F2's edge. If anything it confirms F2's alpha is pure **slug/timing
  selection**, not a basis condition observable in perp tickers.
- F2 remains undeciphered. Remaining §5 levers (not basis): Polymarket **CLOB WS event tape** (sub-second
  maker arrivals), **Polygon mempool** (tx-lag), **wallet behavior graph**. None buildable from canonical.
- Caveat: this OOS window (May 30→Jun 4) post-dates F2's actual on-chain fires (May 10-16, no basis data
  there), so this tests "is basis a profitable selector for the F2 trigger," not "did F2 literally condition
  on basis." Decoding the latter needs a fresh Alchemy chain pull of the two wallets in the basis window —
  separate effort, lower priority given the trigger itself is edgeless OOS.

## Addendum — "fade the dislocation" follow-up (the 6.7%-WR → 93%-fade idea)
Operator flagged: if the dislocated trigger picks win only ~7%, FADING it wins ~93% → maybe a contrarian
directional edge. Tested properly (`f2_fade_dislocation_2026_06_04.py`): build the opposite-side trade with
REAL `up_ask`/`dn_ask` + 0.07 winner-only fee, baseline = market-implied prob = favorite token price.
- Fading = buying the FAVORITE (~$0.70 token); the trigger systematically buys the cheap underdog (entry ~$0.31).
- **True mispricing = fadeWR − favorite_price = +2 to +4pp** (NOT the +42pp a first-pass mislabeled baseline
  showed; the favorite's implied prob is its price ~0.70, not 1−price).
- Full set: +$0.034/tr, t=2.84, CI[+0.011,+0.057] — but this is **clustering-inflated** (5 fires/slug).
- **Slug-deduped (1 fire/slug, n=998): +$0.038/tr, t=1.50, CI[−0.012,+0.089] — includes 0.**
- **Not basis-specific:** deduped dislocated p70 +$0.074 (t=1.59) vs calm +$0.030 (t=0.82); both CIs include 0.
- **DSR prob 0.000, not significant.** The CB-p80 "93% WR" cell is n=15, CI[−0.054,+0.397] = noise.
- Verdict: a faint **favorite-longshot tilt** exists (favorite wins slightly more than priced) but is NOT
  significant after declustering, NOT DSR-significant, and NOT driven by basis dislocation. WR≠edge confirmed
  again. Not tradeable on this data; could be revisited as its own favorite-longshot study with a longer window.
