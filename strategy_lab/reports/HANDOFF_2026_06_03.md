### 2. ⭐ THE REAL DISCOVERY: shadow ≠ live because of PER-HOST PARITY divergence at decision boundaries
The most important new finding. Backtest/shadow WR does NOT predict live, because the shadow and the live
engines read slightly different inputs and the strategies trigger at a boundary, so marginal slots flip:
- **Sniper sleeves (spread gate):** both read `ws_mirror`, but two INDEPENDENT WS BookMirror connections show
  different spreads on thin books at the exact fire ms (prime suspect: shadow path's `TV_POLY_PAPER_BOOK_CACHE_TTL=1`
  serving a ≤1s-stale, tighter snapshot). Slug `1780442400`: shadow spread 0.02 → fired DOWN @0.21; live 0.05 →
  rejected. (`ETH_SHADOW_FIRES_LIVE_DOESNT_BOOKSOURCE_2026_06_02.md`).
- **momo_v2 (ret_2m gate):** identical threshold (0.00151) but each host computes `ret_2m_at_signal` from its
  own Binance feed/bar timing → boundary slots flip fire/no-fire AND direction → hosts place DIFFERENT slots
  (`SOL_MOMO_V2_LIVE_VS_SHADOW_2026_06_02.md`).
- On NON-boundary, tight-book slugs the two agree exactly (eth sniper: 11/11 same slug/dir/price within 1–2¢,
  `ETH_LIVE_VS_SHADOW_SAME_TRADES_2026_06_02.md`).

**Implication:** judge strategies by the LIVE wallet, not shadow/backtest. **Fix for TV agent:** compute the
gate inputs (book spread, ret_2m + its rolling threshold) from the SAME fresh feed/snapshot the live host uses.
