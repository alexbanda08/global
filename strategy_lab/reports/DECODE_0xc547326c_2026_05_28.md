## 6. Mechanism Hypothesis

The wallet appears to operate as follows:

1. **Trigger condition:** Market opens a BTC 5m slug; at some point early (0–120s in), the wallet observes the Polymarket book showing one side at >50%
2. **Direction decision:** Uses cl_basis (binance spot vs chainlink oracle spread) as tiebreaker or primary signal:
   - cl_basis < ~14 bps → the market-favored side confirms Up
   - cl_basis ≥ ~14 bps → the market-favored side confirms Down
   (Or equivalently: high binance premium over chainlink = bet Down)
3. **Entry:** Buys the market-favored side (px > 0.5) early in the window
4. **Hold to resolution:** Standard CLOB hold, no early exit

**Why does this work?** The edge is NOT in predicting something the market hasn't priced — it's in:
- Filtering to high-conviction market states (px > 0.5) that resolve correctly 90%+ of the time
- Firing early enough that the outcome hasn't yet fully resolved into the price
- The cl_basis direction rule has genuine predictive power for which side the market will ultimately price at > 0.5 within the next few minutes

This is distinct from "late entry at near-certain outcome" (which would require entry_px > 0.85). The wallet fires at px ~0.61 median — the market has a 61% lean, but outcomes resolve correctly 90% of the time. This 30pp outperformance is real alpha.
