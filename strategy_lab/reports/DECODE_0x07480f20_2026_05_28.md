## 4. Edge Structure: Two-Tier Analysis

The 75.6% WR decomposes into two very different regimes:

| Tier | n | WR | Mean entry_px | Edge |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| High-px (>0.80) | 324 (40%) | **96.9%** | 0.892 | +7.7pp |
| Low-px (≤0.80) | 492 (60%) | **61.6%** | 0.530 | +8.6pp |

**High-px fires (entry > 0.80):**
- Fired **later** in the window (median offset 194s vs 124s)
- These are near-resolved outcomes — wallet enters when market is already priced at 89% certainty
- ret_3m direction precision jumps to 78.1% for this tier (momentum is aligned with near-certain outcome)
- WR 96.9% vs implied 89.2% = still genuine edge, but on a compressed spread

**Low-px fires (entry ≤ 0.80):**
- ret_3m direction precision = 49.8% — **barely above coin flip**
- WR 61.6% vs implied 53.0% = +8.6pp edge, statistically significant (p=0.00008)
- Edge exists but direction rule does NOT explain it for this tier

**Key insight:** The high WR is partly a "harvesting nearly-certain outcomes" strategy. The wallet enters high-implied-prob markets late in the window. The remaining edge on the lower-px tier (8.6pp over implied) suggests the direction call is marginally better than the market's current pricing, even when momentum precision is low — possibly picking up on cross-token order flow or oracle basis not visible in our features.

## 6. Deployability Verdict

**REPRODUCIBLE with caveats. Deploy-cautious.**

### What is reproducible:
1. **Direction rule (ret_3m > 0):** 61% precision, chi2 p<0.0001, WR improvement +15pp when aligned. This is the core signal.
2. **Enhanced rule (ret_3m + cl_basis):** 80.2% precision on 44.6% of slugs. cl_basis adds independent info (not correlated with ret_3m). Deployable as a direction filter.
3. **High-px harvesting:** Enter near-certainty slugs (entry_px > 0.80) late in window (~194s in). WR 96.9%. Low complexity, low risk.

### What is NOT reproducible:
1. **Slug selection:** Indiscriminate — no filter identified. Must enter all slugs or use a random/vol-based filter.
2. **Win prediction:** Cannot identify winning fires ex-ante. No stop-loss signal exists.
3. **Data window is narrow:** 8 days, 2026-05-20 to 2026-05-27. Cannot assess regime robustness.

### Risk factors:
- **Edge partially mechanistic:** high-px tier is "entering near-resolved markets" — this edge may compress if Polymarket pricing becomes more efficient or liquidity dries up.
- **Low-px tier edge (8.6pp):** statistically real but direction precision is coin-flip. EV depends on correct implied-prob calibration by the wallet (they buy at 53¢ and win 61.6% — that's +8.6pp but low per-trade PnL).
- **102 fires/day volume:** this is a bot trading every ~15min across all BTC 5m slots — replication at this rate requires production-grade automation.

### Recommended replication spec:
```
1. Every BTC 5m slot open:
   - Compute ret_3m and cl_basis_bps at ws_s (slot_start - 300s)
   - If entry_px > 0.80: enter in direction of ret_3m (harvest near-certain)
   - Else if ret_3m>0 AND cl_basis < 11.68: enter Up
   - Else if ret_3m<0 AND cl_basis > 11.68: enter Down
   - Else: skip
2. Hold to resolution (no early exit)
3. Stake: match wallet's $1-2 notional per fire
```
Expected precision on directional call: ~75-80% on filtered set, ~61% on all-slug baseline.
