# Vol regime & Hurst exponent as signals (2026-05-26)

Test whether realized-vol regime and Hurst exponent predict which sleeves work
on Polymarket BTC/ETH/SOL up-down markets. Hypothesis: S6 spike works in HIGH
vol, S1.5 VWAP works in LOW vol. H > 0.5 = trending, < 0.5 = mean-reverting.

**Window:** Apr 30 → May 22 2026 (22 days). **Universe:** 190,170 5m + 50,712 15m
fires from `hybrid_fire_universe_*.parquet`. **Fee model:** LegacyConfig (2% on
profit only — verified production rule).

---

## 1 — Panel build summary

- **Realized vol panel** on 1s binance closes (`realized_vol_1s.parquet`, 5,497,531 rows):
  `rv_{60,300,900,3600}s` stdev of 1s log-returns; `rv_ratio_60_to_3600` as
  vol-of-vol; `rv_pct_24h` rolling 24h percentile of `rv_300s`;
  `vol_regime` 0/1/2 from 33/67 percentile cuts.
- **Garman-Klass vol** on 5m + 15m bars (`gk_vol_{5m,15m}.parquet`):
  `gk_sigma = sqrt(0.5*ln(h/l)² - (2 ln 2 - 1)*ln(c/o)²)` plus 12-bar MA.
- **Hurst exponent** at every fire, R/S method with Anis-Lloyd bias correction,
  numba-JIT, parallel. Windows 100s / 300s / 900s of 1s log-closes ending
  before `fire_us`. Sanity: AR(+0.7) → H ≈ 0.65, AR(−0.7) → H ≈ 0.30, RW → H ≈ 0.50.
- **Output files:**
  - `data/v4/canonical/_results/realized_vol_1s.parquet`
  - `data/v4/canonical/_results/gk_vol_{5m,15m}.parquet`
  - `data/v4/canonical/_results/vol_hurst_at_fire_{5m,15m}.parquet`
- Panel-build time: 65 s total (1s RV: 34s, GK: 2s, Hurst+augment: 29s).
- NaN rate at fires: 4.6 % (boundary windows before 1h of history); 0.02 % for the
  top-7 sleeve sub-panel — negligible.

## 10 — Caveats

1. **Hurst on short windows is noisy.** Even with Anis-Lloyd bias correction,
   the standard deviation of H at w=100s on a pure random walk is ±0.094;
   at w=300s ±0.055. Real signal is large enough to swamp this (e.g. ETH s6
   shows +$22 vs −$13 between H≥0.6 and H<0.4 buckets), but small-sample
   bucket cells (n<50) should be ignored. Used 300s as default — best balance.
2. **SOL Hurst is structurally low.** Only 0.5 % of SOL fires have H≥0.6 on
   300s windows. The "trending SOL" bucket is too thin for actionable sleeve
   conditioning at this horizon. Use H<0.4 (mean-reverting) as the bulk SOL
   regime.
3. **`vol_regime_high` and `g_vol_expanding` overlap heavily** — both flag the
   same fast-vol environments. Their gate lifts on top sleeves are nearly
   identical. Counting them as independent inflates the new-sleeve count;
   #1 and the duplicates (`g_rv_with`, `g_vol_expanding`) tell the same story.
4. **Vol regime hypothesis CONFIRMED for S6 GATED sleeves only.** The BASE S6
   strategy (no other gates) does NOT improve in high vol (44 % WR vs 46 %).
   The cci/stoch/rf/ema50/ribbon stack is what turns the high-vol regime
   profitable — vol regime alone is a refinement, not a primary signal.
5. **S1.5 / S15 base in low vol** is NOT supported. The S15 (VWAP) base
   strategy is breakeven across all regimes (~67-70 % WR, −$0.01 to −$0.04 dpt).
   The S15 sleeve also wins more in HIGH vol once gated. The original
   intuition seems wrong for this universe.
6. **OOS window is 6.4 days** (16 IS / 6.4 OOS split). Some sleeves with
   reasonable IS lifts had only ~20-50 OOS fires — too few to certify.
7. **Fees: LegacyConfig (2 % on profit only)** — production-equivalent.
   Switching to the canonical "real curve" (0.07 × p × (1-p) on both legs)
   would reduce all dpt numbers by ~$0.40 per trade; the ranking of sleeves
   would not change since the fee penalty is direction-symmetric.
