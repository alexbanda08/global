# Z-contra fair-dip backtest on 5m crypto up-down — 2026-05-23 03:23 UTC

Port of `mlmodelpoly/src/strategies/z_contra_fav_dip_hedge.py` to our
28-day BTC/ETH/SOL 5m chainlink-resolved universe. Buys the UNDERDOG
when binance Z-disagrees with PM favorite AND a dip is observed on
the favorite's PM mid.

## Mechanics

- decision_offset_s: { 30, 60, 90, 120 } seconds after slot_start.
- fair_up = Phi(z) Black-Scholes UP-probability at decision time.
- sigma_per_sqrt_sec = std of 1s log-returns over prior 900 bars.
- z_score = (fair_up - pm_up_mid) / max(0.01, sigma * sqrt(tau)).
- favorite = arg max(pm_up_mid, pm_down_mid); underdog = the other.
- DIP rule: pm_favorite_mid_now < pm_favorite_mid_lb * (1 - DIP_BPS/10000).
- Entry: favorite=UP & z < -Z_THRESH & up_dipped  -> BUY DOWN;
         favorite=DOWN & z > +Z_THRESH & down_dipped -> BUY UP.
- Underdog mid must be >= 0.20 (mlmodelpoly UND_PRICE_MIN).
- Fill via engine_v2.fill_at_book @ LegacyConfig (2%-on-profit), $25, spread filter per asset.
