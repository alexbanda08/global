**Momentum, primarily.** Direction features for Up vs Down:

All major features trend momentum-consistent. Explicitly contrarian fires (ret_3m sign disagrees with held_side): 33% of fires, WR=0.800 — slightly HIGHER than momentum fires (WR=0.700), but not significantly at n=15.

**Early-entry verdict:** The early population (offset~11s) IS early-momentum — enters fast on ema9_slope signal before the 15m market prices the move. This explains cheap entry (0.44–0.83) and is the most cleanly decodable sub-population.

## 6. Deployability Assessment

### What's decodable:
1. **EARLY-MOMENTUM rule:** fire within first ~60s of 15m slot, direction = ema9_slope_bps sign, prefer UTC 20-23, prefer lower rv_15m. Agreement: ~91.7% on early pop (n=12).
2. **Entry filter:** entry_px ≥ 0.50 strongly predicts win (WR=0.885). Do NOT follow sub-0.50 entries.
3. **Slug filter:** UTC 20-23 preference; avoid high rv_15m slugs.

### What's NOT decodable:
- End-of-slot (>700s) direction rule — no clean signal found. 69.6% Down but this may be systematic (not momentum-based).
- Slug selection criterion beyond utc_hour + rv_15m (what triggers engagement on some slugs but not others at same hour/vol is unclear).
- Whether the WR edge survives on px≥0.5 entries once entry_px selection is neutralized (wallet may have access to better book info than canonical features capture).

### Binomial test:
- Overall n=45 WR=0.733: p=0.0012 (one-sided vs 0.5) — statistically above chance
- px≥0.5 tier n=26 WR=0.885: p<0.001
- px<0.5 tier n=19 WR=0.526: p=0.50 (not significant)

### Deployability verdict: **EXPERIMENTAL — DO NOT DEPLOY LIVE**

- n=45 over 12 days is insufficient for deployment confidence
- Early-momentum rule (n=12) needs 100+ fires for reliable parameter estimates
- Direction agreement ~67-92% on small sub-groups inflates true edge estimate
- Two-population structure suggests wallet may have signals not in canonical features (e.g. L2 book shape, options flow, on-chain data)
- Minimum threshold before serious consideration: 200+ fires across 60+ days

### If following as shadow:
- Shadow-follow ONLY early fires (offset < 60s): ema9_slope direction, UTC 20-23 window, rv_15m < 3.5 bps
- Apply strict entry_px ≥ 0.50 filter (skip sub-0.5 entries — expect ~WR 0.53 there)
- Expected shadow WR: unclear, but early-momo + px≥0.5 intersection likely higher than 73%
