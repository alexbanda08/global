## 3. What A, B, C Each Missed

### A: +$15,749 (handoff §A)
**Formula used:** `cash_in_wallet + withdrawals - deposits`  
= `19,734 (Jun 11 tape balance) + ~6,000 (approx withdrawn) − 9,985 (deposit) = ~15,749`

**What it missed:**
1. Used **chain tape balance at Jun 11 15:07**, not current cash ($20,639). Cash grew +$905 from Jun 11-12 trading.
2. **Not the correct PnL formula.** The equity formula `cash + withdrawn − deposited` gives the net change in wealth, but requires precise classification of every outflow as fill vs withdrawal. The c417 ($6,100 in) and 4cd00e ($6,201 out) appear to be his own cross-wallet transfers (same amounts, same dates, near-zero net). When included naively they distort the result.
3. The correct equity identity would be: `20,639 + 64.61 (pos_value) + 6,201 (4cd wd) − 9,985 (deposit) = 16,920` — still not LB because **LB is not the equity formula** (it's a trading-only metric, not capital-adjusted).

**Root cause:** LB is Polymarket's internal sum of `(redeem − cost)` across all positions. It does **not** subtract the deposit or add withdrawals. It is a pure trading performance metric, not a portfolio equity.

### B: +$9,749 (reconcile.py)
**Formula used:** `total_USDC_in − deposits_from_pUSD_contract − total_USDC_out` (naive chain ERC20 net)

**What it missed:**
1. **NegRisk pUSD cycling:** In Polymarket negRisk markets, when he buys Up at price p, the wallet sends `p × shares` pUSD to `e111`. But the chain also records `0x0 → wallet: (1-p) × shares` pUSD as the other-side's collateral return. These two flows appear independently in the ERC20 transfer log. The naive formula (total_in − total_out) double-counts both sides of this circular flow, yielding a net that reflects neither the real cost nor the real income.
2. **The 0x0 pUSD mints (1,131,134.54 to wallet)** are NOT income — they are internal negRisk collateral recycling that appears in total_in and partially cancels with the e111 payments in total_out, leaving only the true net cost (≈ price × shares).

**Correct formula:** `REDEEM + REBATE − all_fill_costs` (Polymarket internal) = $21,742.

### C: +$21,742 LB (API, fresh) — CORRECT
Polymarket's internal cumulative realized PnL from all settled positions since inception.  
**Does NOT deduct deposits or withdrawals.** It is a pure trading metric.
