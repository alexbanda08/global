# R5 Gates (microprice / Lee-Mykland / Hawkes) Overlay on R4 15m Sleeves

**Date:** 2026-05-26
**Window:** May 1 → May 25, 2026 (R2 = Train+Val = May 1-21, Lockbox = May 22-25)
**Universe:** 19,703 fires (12,492 R2_train_val + 7,211 lockbox), 3 assets (BTC/ETH/SOL), 8 offset bins
**Splits:** Train: ts < May 15, Val: May 15 → May 22, Lockbox: May 22 → May 26
**Fee model:** Legacy 2%-on-profit (production parity)
**Outcome truth:** Chainlink

## TL;DR

R5 gates **do not add edge above R4 trend_slope** at the SUM-of-lockbox level.
Every R5 filter removes fires; some lift WR/DPT on tiny subsets but never enough to compensate.

Best compound found:
- **POOL 480-600 + g_trend_slope_with + g_mp_no_extreme&g_mp_with&g_hawkes_imbalance_with**
- Lockbox: n=34, WR 94.1%, $/tr +$9.40, sum $319.5, bootstrap p=0.000.
- DPT lift vs R4 baseline +$4.95, WR lift +18.5 pp, but n_kept = 7.3%.

Best by absolute lockbox sum among lift-positive hybrids:
- **POOL 120-240 + g_trend_slope_with&g_vwap_ge_50_le_85 + g_lm_low_stat&g_hawkes_imbalance_with**
- Lockbox: n=55, WR 90.9%, $/tr +$8.92, sum $490.8, p=0.000.
- DPT lift +$2.82, WR lift +11.3 pp, sum delta -$1,211 (still profitable, just less).

**Verdict:** R4 trend_slope already captures the bulk of edge. R5 microstructure features
mostly redundant. Do NOT replace any of the 178 R4 deployables with an R5 hybrid.
Use R5 stacks only if optimising for higher WR / lower drawdown at the cost of throughput.

## 4. KILL Gate Effectiveness (`g_lm_extreme_against`) — Task 4

Result: **gate killed 0/19,703 fires** across all top 25 R4 sleeves.

Only 16 extreme jumps were detected in the panel (`lm_is_jump_extreme=True`), and in ALL 16 cases
the jump direction matched the bet direction (vwap_since_open sign picker already aligned).
The KILL gate has no actionable signal in this regime.

| | n | WR | DPT | Sum |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Baseline (all 25) | 6,657 | 79.3% | $10.11 | $59,976 |
| After g_lm_extreme_against | 6,657 | 79.3% | $10.11 | $59,976 |
| Removed | 0 | — | — | $0 |

Detail: of the 16 extreme jumps, 13/16 won (WR 81%, mean PnL +$3.69). 3 losses occurred when
the market reversed within the 5-15 min window. None showed a "jump against direction" pattern.

**Conclusion:** Lee-Mykland extreme-jump events are too rare at 5s sampling, and when they occur
they reinforce — not contradict — the trend_slope direction picker. KILL gate is moot here.

## 6. Strict 3-Way Validation (Task 5)

- All 20 had lockbox p=0.000 (deeply significant).
- BUT note: every passing combo was a SUBSET of the R4 baseline — they passed because the

## 7. Caveats

1. **Lee-Mykland 5s panel is too sparse for instant signal at 15m fire_us.** Only 0.5% of fires
   have a `g_lm_jump_05` event in their nearest 60s. Anchoring 5s LM stat at a single 15m fire
   point yields high variance. A volatility-weighted LM aggregator over the prior 5min window
   would be a more reliable feature.

2. **Hawkes panel covers only May ~1 onward**, while R2 starts at May 1 — so coverage gap (~36% NaN)
   means 1/3 of fires get NaN→False on Hawkes gates. Filling-forward from same-asset λ
   would recover most.

3. **`g_lm_extreme_against` KILL gate is moot** — only 16 extreme jumps in 32d and all aligned with
   direction picker. To be useful would require a richer threshold scheme (e.g. "any 5s |L|>3
   in last 60s opposed to dir" — but this slips into PEEK territory).

4. **HY cross-exchange basis (`hy_cb_with_dir`) was not built for 15m fires.** Agent EE / xchg
   basis panel covers 5m only as of this run.

5. **Microprice gates are heavily correlated** with R4 `g_tr_stack_with` / `g_di_with` and with
   Hawkes lambda imbalance. The "mp_with + hawkes_imbalance_with" combo is essentially saying
   the same thing twice.

6. **No new R4-grade winners discovered.** R5 overlay on R4 trend_slope sleeves does NOT produce a
   compound that beats R4 alone on absolute PnL. R5 gates may still serve as risk-overlay
   (drawdown-control) but not as additional alpha.
