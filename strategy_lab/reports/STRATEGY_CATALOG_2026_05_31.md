## Explicitly Disproven Dead-Ends (do not retry)

1. **Symmetric maker-arb / pair-arb on up-down markets**: structural adverse selection confirmed by Glosten-Milgrom, confirmed empirically (−$0.41 to −$3.63/slug uncensored). The earlier +$4.44 figure was survivorship bias — losers never logged a REDEEM event.
2. **No-chase merge-arb**: tested with n=10,565 slugs — CI entirely below zero on every asset/TF/budget combination.
3. **Momentum / EMA / ret60 / strike directional taker**: market is informationally efficient — WR always ≈ entry price; 0/35 cells pass G1+G4.
4. **Order-flow imbalance (VPIN, CVD, flow follow/fade)**: 48% directional accuracy; adds noise in ML features; market has priced the information.
5. **RSI/MACD/CCI signal gates**: zero-ops — adding or removing them has no impact OOS.
6. **Complete-set lock/hedge ("buy-wait-lock")**: UP/DOWN asks sum pinned at ~1.01–1.02; realistic pair cost ~$1.09; lockable fraction = 0.0000 at any latency.
7. **|basis_bps| > 12 fires**: reverses to −EV (WR 56%, −$4.17/tr). Hard cap required.
8. **SOL for oracle-lag taker**: net drag (t=−0.5); no passing cell; excluded from LAGV2.
9. **Oracle-snipe taker (late-slot)**: entry price ~$0.986 requires WR 98.6% >> actual 87% accuracy.
10. **Sum < $1 atomic take-both arb**: only 0.004–0.13% book-time; needs sub-100ms colo; UP+DOWN vwap sum ~1.30 on real books.
11. **Multivariate ML (all features vs price)**: GBM 64.7% vs price 65.7%; market is a better predictor than any combination of our features.
12. **Cyclops Telegram signals bot**: true WR 52.1% (coin flip); −$0.42/$25; advertised 85% fabricated.
13. **Price-floor stop-loss**: realizes recoverable noise dips, negative EV.
14. **CEX–CEX latency races, MEV sandwich, microwave/colo**: sub-millisecond races we structurally lose from Ireland.
15. **Pairs/cointegration, vol-arb, funding-carry, index arb, merger arb**: no short-sell of Poly shares; slugs resolve independently; no futures leg available.
