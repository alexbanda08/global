## How to read
- **paired_pct** = 2 × min(up,dn) / (up+dn) per slug — 1.0 = perfectly hedged, 0 = pure directional
- **leftover_on_winner_pct** = of slugs with >10-share residual, what % end up on chainlink winner. 50% = no alpha
- **selection_rate** = N slugs traded / N slugs available in active window+asset+timeframe
- **ratio_traded_to_skipped** (vol) = mean 120s pre-slug vol for traded / skipped (1.0 = no filter)
