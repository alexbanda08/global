## Net: Survivors vs Dead vs Untested

### SURVIVING (HELPS — use these)

1. **Intra-window EXIT-SCALP at +60s / TP0.65 / entry_vwap<0.55** — the only new edge deployed. +$2.56–$2.98/tr, walk-forward t=6.33, p=0.0000 direction permutation. NEEDS ≥200 forward fires before live capital.
2. **Binance-reversal stop ≥10bps** (lag-taker only) — risk-adjusted winner: −32% maxDD, Sharpe +7%, −$0.36/tr EV cost. Information-driven (not noise-driven).
3. **Confidence-proportional sizing** — +17% Sharpe vs flat-$25, +$0.63/tr. Do NOT use naive Kelly ×4 (top delta bucket is negative WR).
4. **SELL_REVERT_5 / HEDGE_REVERT_5** — consistent +0.5–2.0/tr improvement across all assets/TFs vs HOLD when binance reversal is the trigger (signal-based, not price-floor).
5. **HEDGE_LATE (bid < 0.6×entry, last 30s) on MARGINAL sleeves only** — ~8× base PnL on btc_5m_parent15m_notrang (+$39), +$6 on btc_15m_vwapprem. Walk-forward robust. DO NOT apply to >75% WR winners.
6. **Liquidity-aware sizing caps** (BTC ≤$250 soft, ETH ≤$50, SOL ≤$10–15) — discipline prevents slippage swamping edge at scale.
7. **½-Kelly sizing** — documented as single biggest fleet risk reduction; 4×Kelly = ruin guarantee.
8. **BTC_15m sniper SL=50%** (sleeve-specific) — +171% PnL on that specific sleeve. Not general.

### DEAD (HURTS — avoid)

1. **Fixed price-floor stop-loss** — hurts at every threshold (−8% to −44% PnL). Locks noise dips as losses on binary markets.
2. **Trailing stop (10–20%)** — always worse than hold. Fires on noise reversals mid-window.
3. **HEDGE_LATE on winner sleeves (>75% WR)** — clips recovering winners; live _H: all 21 hedges on losers, salvage only 16%.
4. **SELL_FLOOR (bid threshold without signal)** — arbitrary; degrades PnL.
5. **BUY_WAIT_HEDGE complete-set lock** — naive form negative; UP+DOWN sum ~1.30, no lock available.
6. **CUSUM gate for scalp** — added, then dropped (DEAD — not predictive).
7. **Physics vol-regime gate (dist_abs/speed_abs)** — asset-selection artifact; no within-asset lift.

### INCONCLUSIVE / OPEN

1. **ORACLE_CUT (confirmed chainlink reversal)** — CI just barely includes 0. Positive sign on losers but not walk-forward proven. "Right answer for losers is KILL not patch."
2. **SELL_TRAIL at very loose levels (30–40%)** — untested at loose thresholds; could overlap with SELL_REVERT_5 behavior.
3. **TP@0.97 (near-resolution lock)** — approximately breakeven; fee savings ≈ upside surrendered. Not tested with 0.07-curve winner-only fee model.
4. **Forward OOS for scalp exit** — n=76, t=−0.03. Need ≥200 live forward fires to confirm or refute.
5. **HEDGE_LATE on SOL/ETH marginal sleeves** — confirmed on BTC; ETH L25 coverage was 0 at time of study. Not yet tested.
6. **Partial scale-out (e.g. sell 50% at +30s, hold 50% to resolution)** — mentioned as open idea; not formally backtested.
