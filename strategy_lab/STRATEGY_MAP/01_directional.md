### 5 Most Notable DEAD Ideas
1. **Momentum (sig_ret5m) + all variants** — The original strategy. Signal is statistically real (p<0.0005) but entry vwap fully prices it: net-negative after taker fee. The capstone finding: Polymarket CLOB is efficient vs all available pre-window price signals.
2. **Directional WR Scan (8 signals × 6 markets)** — Exhaustive multivariate sweep including mom_ema, mom_ret60, mom_strike, with OOS model. G3 passes everywhere but blind taker always net-negative. Closed the investigation.
3. **BSM / N(d2) Fair-Value** — The only theoretically-grounded alternative. Collapsed because at 5–15m horizons N(d2) = sign(spot−strike) (trivially = momentum in disguise); CLOB price is already a better option pricer.
4. **VPIN + Polymarket CVD direction (B1/C4)** — Classic priced-in trap: 67–89% WR but $/tr −$0.62 to −$1.03. The intra-window flow move is already in the ask price. High WR ≠ edge.
5. **Multi-venue lead-lag (Coinbase/Kraken/OKX/HL-perp)** — Tested as the #1 whitespace item in May-31 plan. Binance-1s already optimal; alternatives are 1MIN bars (too coarse). No upgrade path unless HL 1s tick data is collected.
