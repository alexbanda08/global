## Per-strategy notes

**A1 — Donchian + ATR trailing stop.** Robust across coins on 4h. ETH is the standout (OOS Sharpe 3.3, beats buy-hold 5.6×). N=50 + ATR=1.5 is the sweet spot — short N=20 gets chopped; N=100 leaves PnL on the table. 1d cells generally have n<50 so are excluded from primary judgement.

**A2 — EMA-stack score.** Computed stack score range is [−4, +4] (close vs EMA21, EMA21 vs EMA50, EMA50 vs EMA100, EMA100 vs EMA200). Threshold ±3 catches "strong alignment" without being too rare. BNB/SUI/ADA 4h beat their respective buy-and-hold benchmarks; the win-rate is low (24%) but profit factor is the cushion (PF=1.48 on BNB).

**A3 — ADX-gated EMA trend.** The ADX30 + signal-flip exit variant on ETH 1h shows the highest trade frequency (n=527) and OOS Sharpe 2.06, but does NOT beat BH (0.59) on Sharpe. Useful as a higher-frequency overlay. ATR-trail variant uniformly worse than signal-flip — the trail kicks too early in mid-trend.

**A4 — Range-Filter dir-flip.** Only 22 cells (one variant × 11 assets × 2 tfs). 1d direction-change exit is too rare (n<50 typically). 4h breaks down on BTC/ETH/SOL — the rf_dir flips too often on 4h (avg_bars_held = 2.0). 1d looks promising (LINK 1d Sharpe 2.24, SUI 1d 3.71) but n_OOS is sub-20. NOT recommended.

**A5 — Cyclops AND-gate filter on Donchian.** Gating improves BTC/ETH 4h: BTC Sharpe 0.95 → 1.26, ETH 0.69 → 1.25 with similar PnL but fewer trades. SOL flips negative — Cyclops levels axis is noisy on SOL. The gate cuts ~25-30% of fires and concentrates them in regime-aligned breakouts. Useful overlay, not a standalone strategy.

## Caveats
1. Funding ignored for BNB/ADA/DOGE/XRP/SUI/TON — real HL deploy would accrue funding. Likely ~$0.05/trade haircut for typical funding rates.
2. ETH dominance in the top-10 may reflect post-2023 alt-relative-strength; rerun on rolling windows to test regime robustness before sizing up.
3. A4 (Range Filter) used `rf_dir` from panel for BTC/ETH/SOL but EMA-slope proxy for other coins — not apples-to-apples.
4. The HL backtest uses next-bar-open fills (no book-walk slippage modeled); $250 notional × 1x is below any depth limit on HL majors, so kline-open is a fair approximation.
